# WordPack privacy policy

*Effective 25 September 2026*

WordPack ("the app") is a vocabulary learning app for Android, published by gauravpwagh. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

## Summary

WordPack does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal information. Everything you put into the app stays on your device.

## Information the app handles

- **Your word lists.** When you import a file, the app reads it and saves the words, parts of speech and definitions in a database on your device.
- **Your study data.** Learning progress, tags, categories and settings are saved in the same on-device database.

None of this is sent anywhere. The app has no account system, contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting tools, and does not request the internet permission, so it cannot connect to the internet.

## Files you choose

The app only opens a file when you pick it with the system file picker, and only reads that file. When you export a backup, the app creates a file and hands it to the destination you choose (for example, a folder on your device or an app in the share sheet). What happens to the file after that is governed by the destination you picked.

## Permissions

WordPack requests no special Android permissions.

## Deleting your data

You can delete a wordlist in the app at any time. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, removes all of its data from your device.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.
